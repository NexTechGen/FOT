Degree programme, programme structure and study areas for the Department of Engineering Technology.

Degree Programme

The Department of Engineering Technology offers a four-year Bachelor of Engineering Technology Honours undergraduate degree comprising eight semesters. Students can pursue one of three specialisations: Automobile Technology, Construction Technology, or Electro Technology.

The published department information describes the programme as designed to comply with Sydney Accord requirements and Sri Lanka Qualifications Framework (SLQF) Level 6.

Compliance with Sydney Accord requirements and SLQF Level 6 is stated as published department information and is not presented here as a claim of accreditation. Confirm current details with the department.

Study areas and specialisations

The department's teaching is organised around three specialisations. Each specialisation has its own dedicated page.

Programme Structure

The semester structure of the four-year Bachelor of Engineering Technology Honours degree.

The first two semesters are common to all Engineering Technology students. Specialisation studies begin in Semester 3 and continue through the remaining years of the four-year degree programme.
